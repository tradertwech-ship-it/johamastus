Admission Requirements

Please review the required prerequisites and documents before submitting your application.

Required Documents

  • PLE / UCE Results Slip or official academic transcripts
  • Copy of Birth Certificate or Valid ID
  • Recommendation letter from previous school head teacher
  • 2 recent passport-size photographs

Academic Qualifications

  • Satisfactory pass grades in core subjects
  • Official leaving certificate from previous institution
  • Passing grade in school entrance evaluation (where applicable)

Age / Class Requirements

  • Senior 1 Entry: Ages 11 – 13 years
  • Senior 5 Entry: Valid UCE pass certificate or equivalent
  • Mid-level Transfers: Progress report cards for previous two terms
